Zone Guide
Zone 1 — Recovery
Very easy, almost guilt-inducing. Great for full recovery days or between hard intervals.
RPE under 5
Zone 2 — Aerobic Endurance
Conversational pace. Feels easy at first, gets harder over long durations. Most of your training lives here.
RPE 5–7
Zone 3 — Tempo
Conversation is difficult. Used in tempo intervals and hill climbs. This is where fitness gets built.
RPE 7–8
Zone 4 — Lactate Threshold
Legs and lungs burning. Hard to sustain for more than an hour. Peak week intervals only.
RPE 8–9
Zone 5 — Anaerobic
Maximum effort. Intervals under 5–6 minutes only. Boosts VO2max and anaerobic threshold.
